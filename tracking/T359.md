# T359 - Surf Ace Node Sun Schedule Appearance

Status: source-safe implementation slice in progress.

## Product Goal

Each Surf Ace appliance node has an explicit timezone and location profile. A real sunrise/sunset calculation derives the environment appearance signal from that profile. The compositor/runtime publishes only environment appearance state; Surf Ace/apps own rendering and styling.

## Stable Requirements

- Node profile: `nodeId`, IANA `timezone`, `latitude`, `longitude`.
- Calculation: compute local-date sunrise and sunset from node latitude/longitude and timezone.
- Appearance:
  - `light` when evaluated time is at or after sunrise and before sunset.
  - `dark` before sunrise or at/after sunset.
  - `unknown` when concrete schedule truth cannot be determined.
- Status/control visibility:
  - `runtime.appearance`
  - `runtime.appearance_source`
  - `runtime.sun_schedule.profile`
  - `runtime.sun_schedule.evaluatedAtUnixSeconds`
  - `runtime.sun_schedule.localDate`
  - `runtime.sun_schedule.sunriseUnixSeconds`
  - `runtime.sun_schedule.sunsetUnixSeconds`
  - `runtime.sun_schedule.nextTransitionUnixSeconds`
  - `runtime.sun_schedule.reason`
- Missing data behavior: no profile fails closed to `unknown` with reason `missing_profile`.
- Invalid data behavior: bad timezone/location or no concrete sunrise/sunset fails closed to `unknown` with observable reason/error.
- Boundary: no app styling policy in compositor.
- Source-safe boundary: no deployment, persistent scheduler, autostart, timer, restart policy, or service enablement without explicit Flynn approval.

## Source Slice
